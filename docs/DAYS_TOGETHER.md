# Days Together — 0.4.0

Press **H** (remappable) or Pause → **Our days together**. The Together tab offers appreciation, breathing and dancing. The Village day tab lists current resident activities and locations. Opening it pauses the clock.

Stay close to your partner on solid ground, with a clear view between you. Both characters animate during a shared moment. Move or press Esc to finish early; opening a menu also ends it. Neither partner is disappointed and there is no cost. Completing a moment creates a journal note and records that day once; repeats remain available.

## Shared places

Pink hearts mark these places on the map after their region is discovered. Approach the small flower marker/mat and press E:

| Place | Moment |
| --- | --- |
| Outside the treehouse, southeast of the entrance | Tea together |
| South bank of the flower-garden pond | Garden date |
| West bank of the lower waterfall pond | Watch the sky |
| Western side of the village park | Picnic |
| Northern edge of the player rice plots | Tend the village together |
| Centre of the market square | Supper at the lantern market, 18:00–00:00 |

Poppy/Finn can invite you to play catch during playtime. Sumi/Ren can invite you to help during work time. These are short shared gestures and conversation, not skill-based minigames. Farming rituals do not consume items or silently alter your planted crops.

## Daily pace

The default day lasts 36 real minutes. Settings → Gameplay & Display offers 12, 24, 36 or 60 minutes. Menus pause time. Children retire at 20:00, most adults at 21:00, and market residents at midnight. House door interactions tell you who lives there and what they are doing. Neighbour interiors are not enterable yet.

Most workers wake at 06:00. Children and late-market residents wake at 07:00. Normal vendor counters open at 07:00 and close at midnight; the player's own fruit stall remains self-service. The lantern market runs 18:00–00:00. The player can stay awake with their partner as long as they like.

Quiet HUD hides the quest reminder. Idle couple conversations can be disabled independently. Reduced motion stops the new swing/ball phase advancement; pose transitions still animate. C switches the playable character after landing; Swan's F/Space/Ctrl flight controls remain.

Existing schema-1 saves remain supported. NPC locations are reconstructed from the saved time; shared memories are optional new world-state keys. Back up important saves before testing development builds.
